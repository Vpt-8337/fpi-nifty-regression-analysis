# fpi-nifty-regression-analysis
In contrast with my original hypothesis, in which I estimated that foreign investment would co-relate to NIFTY returns at least by 40%. This regression model tested the hypothesis of a key factor in Financial Markets, how much do net FPI flows affect the stock market, in consideration of monthly intervals from March 2021 to March 2026 (present) of NIFTY 50 returns and ^VIX, FPI net flows associate with 32.1% (Adjusted R2) of NIFTY 50 returns, according to results, an FPI inflow of 1,00,000 crores is likely associated with a 0.5% return increase, however, p-value of 0.216 for CBOE VIX proves the presence of VIX statistically insignificant, a key question lies, is it accurate to consider VIX for a regression model, as FPI flows may already account for global market risk sentiments. In conclusion, the results do prove that a net inflow of FPI leads to an increase in NIFTY returns, yet, the effect is modest.
